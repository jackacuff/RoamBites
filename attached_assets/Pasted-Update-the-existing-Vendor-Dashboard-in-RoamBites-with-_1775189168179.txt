Refine and reorganize the current dashboard to make it more valuable, more action-oriented, and more focused on helping vendors make money.

GOAL
The vendor dashboard should feel less like a settings panel and more like a business control center. It should clearly guide vendors toward:
- getting found
- getting booked
- promoting themselves
- taking quick daily actions

1. Improve the subscription / membership card
The current subscription card is too passive.

Update the trial / subscription card so it feels more outcome-driven and valuable.

Instead of emphasizing only:
- days left in trial
- price after trial

Shift messaging toward value such as:
- Get more bookings
- Unlock featured placement
- Access advanced social media tools
- Get more event visibility

Change button language from:
- “Subscribe Now”

To something stronger such as:
- “Unlock Pro Features”
- “Upgrade for More Bookings”
- “Go Pro”

Keep trial information visible, but make the card sell outcomes, not just billing.

2. Add a “Grow Your Business” section near the top
Add a new high-visibility section near the top of the vendor dashboard focused on revenue-driving actions.

Suggested section title:
- “Grow Your Business”

This section should surface the most important monetization/growth actions, such as:
- Booking Requests
- Event Opportunities / Spot Marketplace
- Promote Your Truck
- Social Media tools

This section should feel like the most important daily-use area of the dashboard.

3. Move and strengthen the “Share & Promote” area
The existing “Share & Promote” area is important, but it currently feels secondary.

Move it higher on the dashboard, ideally close to or directly below the live status section.

Strengthen the content and framing.

Consider changing it from:
- “Share & Promote”

To something more action-oriented such as:
- “Promote Your Truck Today”

And clarify value:
- Share today’s location
- Post your schedule
- Drive customers to your RoamBites page

Make this feel like a core action, not a footer-style add-on.

The current “Branding & Media” card sounds too much like a basic settings page.

- “Social Media Hub (Pro)”

- Create and post branded content
- Manage your socials
- Drive more customers back to your truck

This section should feel like a premium marketing engine, not just a media upload tool.

5. Improve the live status card
The “You’re Live!” section is strong and should remain prominent.
